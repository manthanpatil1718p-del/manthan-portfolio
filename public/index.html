06 — How We Work

From Raw Footage to Finished Film.

A transparent, reliable 4-step creative pipeline that guarantees stress-free delivery and zero surprises.

01

Footage & Vision

You upload your raw footage via Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer. We discuss target audience, reference edits, pacing style, and music mood.

02

Assembly & Rough Cut

I organize your selects, craft the narrative structure, pace the edits to the rhythm, and share the initial cut for structural validation.

03

Sound Design & Grade

Layering atmospheric foley, whooshes, risers, and impact SFX, followed by full color grading to establish the final cinematic visual mood.

04

Feedback & Master

We review any requested fine-tuning tweaks. Once you are 100% satisfied, you receive full-resolution 4K and optimized web masters ready to publish.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about starting a project, footage transfer, revision rounds, and turnaround times.
